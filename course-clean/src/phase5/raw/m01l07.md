## Dock decision basis
Classify incoming material against the purchase order, the drawing, the specification and the incoming inspection plan. ISO 9001:2015 requires control of externally provided processes, products and services, so the dock record has to show the evidence behind an accept, a quarantine or a reject.

## Quarantine as a controlled hold
Quarantine is a controlled hold on material that is suspect, incomplete or awaiting review. Segregate it physically or hold it in the system, label it with part number, lot, quantity, date and hold reason, and keep it traceable to the receiving transaction. The hold is what keeps the material out of production while the material review board or the quality function assesses risk.

## MRB inputs and dispositions
A material review board reviews the nonconformance report, drawings, measurement data, photos, supplier lot history and downstream process risk. It may accept, rework, sort, use as-is with concession, return or scrap. The disposition names who approved it and what verification is required before release.

## Supplier notification
Notification works when it is specific: part number, lot, purchase order, quantity received, quantity inspected, finding, specification requirement, measured result and evidence. Ask for containment, root cause, corrective action, and replacement or return authorisation according to the contract. "Bad parts" gives the supplier nothing to correct.

## Classification criteria
Accept when the evidence shows conformance to the specified requirements. Quarantine when documentation, packaging, moisture, ESD, shelf life or measurement uncertainty leaves the status unclear. Reject when a confirmed finding affects fit, form, function, safety or a regulatory requirement, or when the quarantine cannot be resolved inside the planned disposition window.

## Record content and traceability
A usable record ties the finding to the lot and to any downstream use: inspector, date, location, measurement method, calibration status, sample size, observed defects, disposition, approvals and closure evidence. For electronic components, record moisture-sensitive device status and verify it against the device datasheet and IPC/JEDEC J-STD-033, because the level drives the bake and floor-life decision.

## Closing the loop with the line
Dock dispositions move the assembly schedule. A released lot goes to the line with its approved status and any special handling notes; a rejected lot triggers substitutes or a schedule change. Close the supplier corrective action only after containment, root cause and an effectiveness check are documented.

## Key takeaways
Hold suspect material under a labelled, traceable quarantine, because a verbal hold nobody can audit protects nothing.
Send the supplier the requirement, the measured result and the evidence, so the corrective action can address the cause.
Record who inspected, with which method and calibration, so the dock decision can be reproduced later.

## Common mistakes
Leaving quarantined material in the stores location with only a paper note :: Segregate it physically or hold it in the system, and label part number, lot, quantity and reason.
Notifying the supplier with a symptom only :: State the requirement, the measurement and the affected reels or units, and ask for the specific action the contract allows.
Closing a corrective action when the replacement arrives :: Keep it open until containment, root cause and an effectiveness check are all documented.

## Glossary
Material review board :: The cross-functional group that dispositions nonconforming material and records the basis for its decision.
Quarantine :: The controlled holding of suspect or incomplete material until a disposition is approved.
Concession :: A written approval to use material that does not meet the stated requirement.
Containment :: Immediate action to stop nonconforming material from moving further into the process or to the customer.
