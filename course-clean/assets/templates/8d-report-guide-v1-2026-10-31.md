# 8D problem report

v1 · 2026-10-31 · serves cx-m14-01

## What it is for

Take a defect from containment to closure in the standard eight disciplines, with the numbers that prove each one.

## Why it looks like this

The m14 case keeps an 8D open because the action improved the rate and did not reach the agreed metric. A report that cannot state its metric cannot be closed either.

## How to fill it in

1. Fill every row. A blank is a finding: it means the evidence was not collected, which is different from evidence that passed.
2. Where a row asks for a number, record the unit and the sample it came from in the same row.
3. The evidence column is for what you attached — a photograph with a scale, a printout, a measurement file. A row with no evidence is an opinion.

## What the worked example shows

The example variant is filled with Board X numbers from case cx-m14-01. It is there to show the shape of a complete record, not to be copied: Board X is a fictional product and its limits are the course's.
