# Incoming inspection checklist

v1 · 2026-10-31 · serves cx-m01-01

## What it is for

Record what was checked on the dock, what was sampled, which lot the sample came from, and what the disposition is — so a quarantine can be explained a year later.

## Why it looks like this

The m01 case turns on a lot that measures fine and cannot be traced: two date codes, labels that do not match the boxes. A checklist that only records measurements would have passed it.

## How to fill it in

1. Fill every row. A blank is a finding: it means the evidence was not collected, which is different from evidence that passed.
2. Where a row asks for a number, record the unit and the sample it came from in the same row.
3. The evidence column is for what you attached — a photograph with a scale, a printout, a measurement file. A row with no evidence is an opinion.

## What the worked example shows

The example variant is filled with Board X numbers from case cx-m01-01. It is there to show the shape of a complete record, not to be copied: Board X is a fictional product and its limits are the course's.

## Reference

ISO 2859-1 (sampling), IPC-6012 (bare-board acceptability). Taught in lesson m01l04.
