# Design feedback pack

v1 · 2026-10-31 · serves cx-m15-01

## What it is for

Turn a production result into a design ask with a number in it and a price on it, so the design team can act on the next revision.

## Why it looks like this

The m15 case ends with three asks, of which one is a rule. The pack has to carry the cost of poor quality, the payback of the change, and the sample the numbers came from.

## How to fill it in

1. Read the **THRESHOLDS** block first. Those are the limits this sheet judges against; if your quality system specifies different ones, change them there and nowhere else.
2. Enter your numbers in the white cells only. The grey cells are formulas and are locked — that is deliberate, so a filled sheet can be checked against the arithmetic rather than trusted.
3. A limit with no source id is a course or control-plan value, not a published requirement. Say so when you quote it.
4. Nothing on the sheet is protected by a password: Review > Unprotect Sheet if you need to change its structure.

## What the worked example shows

The example variant is filled with Board X numbers from case cx-m15-01. It is there to show the shape of a complete record, not to be copied: Board X is a fictional product and its limits are the course's.

## Reference

The plant's own production data; the placement acceptance and the fiducial geometry are Board X's. Taught in lesson m15l04.
